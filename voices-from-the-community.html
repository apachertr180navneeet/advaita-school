Testimonial

Tanya Rupani ShahParent

Hi I’m Tanya Rupani Shah . My daughter has been in Advaita School of Excellence – Parbhani  since its inception. We have had a beautiful journey from pre- primary to now grade 3 . The beauty of this school isn’t only it’s great academics or co-curricular subjects but it’s personalized involvement in the  development of your child from their class teachers’ , specialist teachers’,  co-ordinators and even the Head of school. The holistic approach the school takes is fantastic for the future of tomorrow.

Mansi Maniarparents of Vivaan Maniar - Grade 3A.

Being parents of a child who joined Advaita ORT in the very first year of school, we have seen the school transform and grow to become this very big yet closely knit PORTIS family, that it is today. 

Like all schools today, Advaita ORT too is offering a plethora of co curricular activities throughout the year. But what differentiates PORTIS is that “agency” is given to the students to choose how they would like to showcase their understanding. This has truly helped children to become independent thinkers and take responsibility for their actions from an early age. 

Even in the current pandemic situation, the teachers have worked tirelessly to ensure that there is variety and novelty in the strategies used to take online classroom lessons. Online quizzes and games, competitions, energiser breaks, festival celebrations, virtual displays; just to mention a few; have made this new format of learning – a fun and engaging experience.
